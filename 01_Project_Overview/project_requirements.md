# Functional requirements

1. Store product details, SKU, category, preferred supplier, cost, selling price and reorder level.
2. Store supplier contact and status information.
3. Store warehouse location, capacity and status.
4. Create purchase orders for a supplier and destination warehouse.
5. Store multiple product lines for each purchase order.
6. Record incoming and outgoing stock movements.
7. Maintain current stock for each product-warehouse pair.
8. Report inventory value by warehouse.
9. Identify products requiring replenishment.
10. Analyse supplier purchases and monthly stock movement.
