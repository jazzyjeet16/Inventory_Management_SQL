# Problem Statement

A distributor needs a relational system to track products across suppliers and warehouses. The database records product categories, supplier details, warehouse capacity, purchase orders and line items, current stock by warehouse, and incoming/outgoing stock movements.

## Objectives

1. Maintain consistent master data for products, suppliers, categories and warehouses.
2. Record purchase orders and their product line items.
3. Track stock movement history and derive current warehouse balances.
4. Identify products at or below reorder thresholds.
5. Produce warehouse valuation, supplier purchasing and movement reports.
6. Demonstrate MySQL constraints, indexes, joins, aggregation, views, triggers, transactions and CSV import/export.
